Need help with Blobby’s Woodland Wonder, have a question about ListPull wildlife media, or want to discuss a partnership? Send us a message.

Email us

For general questions, support, privacy requests, or business inquiries, contact:

support@listpull.com

Include these details for support

  • Your device type and operating-system version.
  • The game or ListPull service you are contacting us about.
  • A clear description of the issue, including steps to reproduce it when applicable.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if they help explain the issue.
