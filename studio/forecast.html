The Neubra Electron project uses a dedicated `arima` Node package, training and cached models. This browser demonstration is a new, reduced ARIMA(1,1,0) implementation with an OLS-estimated lagged-difference model. Its displayed interval is a rough teaching illustration—not a calibrated prediction interval. It is not equivalent to Neubra's production research service. Do not use this demo for investment, safety-critical, or operational decisions.
