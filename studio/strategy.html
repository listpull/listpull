02 / Analyze the responses

Equilibrium explorer

When empirical action/payoff data is available, the discovered mean payoff matrix can be loaded here. An equilibrium means neither party benefits by changing its own strategy alone under those assumed payoffs.

Payoffs are illustrative preference scores, not predictions or measured business outcomes. The solver enumerates pure equilibria and a fully mixed 2×2 equilibrium when one exists. Degenerate games can have additional equilibrium continua and are not fully enumerated.
