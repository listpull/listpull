Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Blobby and the game.

Who is Blobby?

Blobby is the cheerful orange woodland alchemist, artist, and builder who guides players through magical floating forest biomes in Blobby's Woodland Wonder.

Is Blobby's Woodland Wonder available on both iOS and Android?

Yes! Blobby's Woodland Wonder is officially available on the Apple App Store for iPhone & iPad, as well as the Google Play Store for Android smartphones and tablets.

What makes Blobby unique compared to other puzzle games?

Blobby combines 3D isometric merge mechanics, 3D cube animal sorting puzzles, flying rescue power-ups, and real-world wildlife inspiration backed by ListPull Media.
